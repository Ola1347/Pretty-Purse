            Star pilot

            Fly your starship, blast the invaders, and survive the waves.

            Score 0Wave 1Lives
            Press Start to launch your starship.

            How to play

            1. Move your ship with the left and right arrow keys, or A and D. On a phone, drag on the game or hold the Left and Right buttons.
            2. Your ship fires on its own. Shoot the invaders before they reach you. Each crash costs a life, and you have 3.
            3. Waves get faster every 18 seconds. Gold tanks take three hits.
            4. Destroyed invaders sometimes drop a gold star (+50 points) or a 2x power-up that doubles your shots for 8 seconds.
            5. Press P to pause. When the game ends, your score turns into sparkle coins.
